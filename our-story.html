How it began

Our Story

Eldeva was created for busy lives, for the moments when you finally get to exhale. Made for the pauses where you slow down and come back to yourself, it began as a family hobby and quietly grew into a small-batch ritual designed to bring calm home. Each scent is crafted to feel familiar: soft, grounding, and quietly comforting.

Today, we hand-pour soy candles, wax melts, and diffusers in small batches, focusing on refined fragrance, clean burns, and gift-ready details. Each piece is designed to soften busy days and turn small moments into rituals worth remembering.

Founder's Note

Eldeva began during a season that demanded resilience. After being made redundant more than once, I started pouring candles at home with my family, a small way to rebuild and create something steady with our own hands. What began as a hobby became our ritual: small-batch home fragrance designed to bring calm into busy lives through soft, grounding, memory-led scent.
JazzFounder of Eldeva
