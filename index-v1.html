Our story

A family kitchen, a few pots of soy wax, and a stubborn belief that a candle should smell just as good on day ninety as on day one.

We pour every Eldeva candle in small batches so we can taste-test each scent the slow way: in our own living rooms. Amber glass, recyclable boxes and fragrance oils strong enough to fill a room even when the wick is cold.
